# Granted

> Granted is an AI-plus-human healthcare navigation service that helps consumers reduce medical bills and manage health insurance. It detects billing errors, finds financial-assistance programs, negotiates discounts, and manages claims across 1,300+ insurers and patient portals on a success-based model (free to use; pay only on savings). Backed by Forerunner Ventures. Granted publishes a consumer app, not a public developer API.
